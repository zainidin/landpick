Privacy Policy

Updated 14 October 2026

The UAE Land website (“we”) helps you choose and reserve land plots in the Emirate of Sharjah, UAE.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the enquiry form: name, phone or WhatsApp, email, selected plots and comment. The site may also store your chosen currency and area units in your browser.

Why

To contact you, send the offer for the plot, answer your questions and arrange a site visit. We do not use your data for other purposes and never sell it.

Who we share it with

Only our sales team sees your data. To select and process a plot it may be shared with the developer or seller of that plot. Enquiries technically pass through the website hosting services and messengers.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your enquiry, and no longer than 3 years after the last contact unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

You can request, correct or delete your data and withdraw your consent. Contact us: WhatsApp +971 55 872 2035 · Telegram @investgcc.

Law

We process data in line with UAE law, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data.
